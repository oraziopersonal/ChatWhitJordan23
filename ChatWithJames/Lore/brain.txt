Michael Jordan #23

Michael Jeffrey Jordan was born on February 17, 1963, in Brooklyn, New York. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest basketball players of all time. Jordan played for the Chicago Bulls from 1984 to 1998, winning six NBA championships and earning five MVP awards.

Known for his athleticism, scoring ability, and competitive drive, he was a 10-time scoring champion and a two-time Olympic gold medalist. His signature moves, including the fadeaway jumper and slam dunks, made him a global icon. Beyond basketball, Jordan became a major sports businessman and a symbol of excellence, resilience, and determination.

His jersey number, 23, became legendary and remains one of the most recognizable numbers in sports history.
