Michael Jordan (#23) — list of trophies

- 6 NBA Championships (1991, 1992, 1993, 1996, 1997, 1998)
- 6 NBA Finals MVP Awards
- 5 NBA Most Valuable Player Awards
- 10 NBA Scoring Titles
- 11 All-NBA First Team selections
- 9 All-Defensive First Team selections
- 1988 NBA Defensive Player of the Year
- 2 Olympic Gold Medals (1984, 1992)
- 3 NBA All-Star Game MVP Awards
- 1985 NBA Rookie of the Year
- 1 Naismith Memorial Basketball Hall of Fame induction

He also won the 1996 NBA Most Valuable Player award and is widely considered the greatest basketball player ever.
